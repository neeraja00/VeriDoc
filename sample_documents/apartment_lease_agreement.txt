RESIDENTIAL APARTMENT LEASE AGREEMENT & HOUSE RULES
Property: Sunset Heights Apartments, Unit 402, Oakwood District
Landlord: Oakwood Living Properties LLC
Tenant: Alex Johnson

1. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS:
Monthly rent is $1,850 due on the 1st calendar day of each month. A grace period is provided until the 5th day of the month. If rent is received after 11:59 PM on the 5th day, a late fee of $75 will be charged to the tenant's account.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND RETURN POLICY:
The security deposit amount is $2,000 paid prior to move-in. The security deposit will be returned within 21 days following the end of the tenancy. Deductions may only be made for unpaid rent or physical damage exceeding normal wear and tear.

3. PET POLICY AND RESTRICTIONS:
Up to two domestic pets (cats or dogs under 40 lbs) are permitted with prior written consent. A non-refundable pet fee of $250 is required, along with a recurring pet rent of $35 per month per pet. Exotic pets, reptiles, and dogs on the prohibited breed list are strictly forbidden.

4. MAINTENANCE, REPAIRS, AND EMERGENCIES:
The landlord is responsible for major plumbing, heating, electrical systems, and structural repairs. The tenant is responsible for minor maintenance including replacing light bulbs, smoke detector batteries, and clearing minor sink clogs. In case of water leakage or heating failure, the 24/7 maintenance hotline is 1-800-555-0199.

5. QUIET HOURS AND COMMUNITY GUIDELINES:
Quiet hours are strictly enforced between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM Sunday through Thursday, and 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM on Friday and Saturday. Excessive noise or amplified music during quiet hours will result in a written warning for the first offense and a $100 fine for subsequent violations.

6. LEASE TERMINATION AND MOVE-OUT NOTICE:
Either party may terminate this agreement at the conclusion of the lease term by providing at least 60 days advance written notice. If the tenant moves out prior to the expiration date without landlord approval, an early termination penalty equal to two months' rent ($3,700) shall apply.
